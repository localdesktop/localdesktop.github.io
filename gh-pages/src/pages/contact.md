# Contact

## Bug reports and feature requests

The fastest way to get help is to [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/localdesktop/localdesktop.github.io/issues). Please include your device model, Android version and what you were doing when the problem happened. The [Logcat guide](/docs/developer/how-to-logcat) explains how to capture logs that help us find the cause.

Before opening a new issue, check the [User Manual](/docs/user/getting-started). Many common problems, such as the app closing in the background on Android 12 and newer, are already explained there.

## Questions and ideas

For general questions, ideas and sharing your setup, use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/localdesktop/localdesktop.github.io/discussions).

## Email

For privacy requests, sponsorship, press or anything that should not be public, email [hongphat.js@gmail.com](mailto:hongphat.js@gmail.com). We usually reply within a few days.

## Contributing

Code and documentation contributions are welcome. Start with [How to build](/docs/developer/how-to-build) and the [Developer Manual](/docs/developer/how-it-works).
